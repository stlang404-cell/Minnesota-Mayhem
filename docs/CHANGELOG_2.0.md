## Completion status (honest)
### Completed
Everything in the table above, verified by tests, the balance harness and the browser journey.

### Partially completed
* **Arsenal size:** 76 abilities, not the 100+ target. I stopped at abilities with a distinct mechanic, coverage-tested in simulation (`scripts/ability-coverage.ts`), rather than pad with filler.
* **Exchange contract types:** match winner, first elimination and a match-length over/under are implemented. **Tournament-champion and promotion-outcome markets are not implemented**, and neither are limit orders (the brief allowed skipping them unless fully done).
* **Imperial Tournament:** a single 16-player bracket (4 heats → final). It is not a multi-day event, and AI-only tournaments have no markets.
* **Rival memory:** AIs use public reputation (VPIP, PFR, aggression, fold-to-raise, showdown bluffs) carried across matches and weighted by familiarity. They do not learn per-opponent betting-size patterns.
* **Divisions** are display-only subdivisions of the rating band; promotion still happens only through trials.
* **Audio:** synthesized effects with volume and mute; still no music.

### Not implemented
* Seasonal championship *events* (seasons are standings-based; the champion is the points leader).
* Portrait customization beyond frames (clothing and hair editing).
* Arena decorations as purchasable placeable items (banners recolor the arena scene instead).

## Verified results (from `docs/BALANCE_REPORT.md`, generated by `npm run balance`)
* **Match length:** 720 AI matches; median 38 hands ≈ 2.9 min at human pace (p90 3.4 min); max 56 hands. Quick Clash averages ~23 hands.
* **Archetypes (10):** every archetype wins 18–34% at every tier tested, except **Discipline at the Imperator tier at 36.5% (n=96, about one standard error above the 36% flag)**. Its top-level Ward was reduced (Vestal Vigil level 3: 4 → 3 Wards); the residual is within sampling noise and is monitored.
* **Combos & fusions:** 24–30.5% against three full archetype builds (control: 27.5%). None dominates.
* **AI 2.0:** 56.0% win share against Version 1 over 400 matches (avg place 2.47 vs 2.53); 67–92% against station/rock/maniac/random baselines; ~0.2 ms per decision.
* **Progression (6 simulated careers × 140 matches):** Plebeian ~15 matches, Gladiator ~36, Centurion ~57; all six reached Consul, two reached Augustus. Trials pass 55% of the time. Median bankroll grows $479 → $974 → $4,168 → $18,175 (after 10/40/80/140 matches), a visible poor-to-wealthy arc.
* **Recovery:** broke players always had a playable match; median 1 free match to afford a ranked entry again.
* **Exchange:** pre-match prices are barely better than uniform (Winner Brier 0.752), because ratings say little about a 4-player knockout. Live prices after hand 20 are clearly informative (Brier 0.717). Value-based simulated traders profit and momentum traders lose. The LMSR loss bound was never violated.
* **Mode economics** (competent sim player at Gladiator, 25 runs each): Ranked +$15.86, Quick +$10.97, High Roller +$64.13, Draft +$27.80, Rival +$58.97, Gauntlet +$48.24 (after re-tuning its cash-out ladder from −$46.80), Tournament −$28.44. The Tournament pays an average entrant 1.125× entry in expectation, so the negative mean reflects a mid-skill player and high variance over 25 runs.
* **World stability (200 days):** rivals 121 → 135 (hard cap 140, retirements); rival wealth +53%, now self-limiting via lifestyle spending (was +108% before the fix); save size ~181 KB; 200 simulated days take ~0.15 s.

## Bugs found and fixed during 2.0 verification
* The tutorial card covered the betting buttons on iPhone, then the speed controls. It now starts collapsed to a corner chip on phones; found by the iPhone journey.
* The coach component kept stale state across screens (it is now keyed per screen).
* The turn indicator rendered as a large gold wedge (unsupported CSS mask); replaced with a timer bar.
* Side pots displayed preflop from unequal blinds; they now appear only when someone is all-in.
* The Gauntlet cash-out ladder had negative expected value at every stage; re-tuned.
* The rival population grew without bound and rival wealth inflated; added a cap and a wealth sink.
* Tutorial cards covered Exchange buttons on desktop in one run; the coach now sits bottom-left, clear of all action buttons.
